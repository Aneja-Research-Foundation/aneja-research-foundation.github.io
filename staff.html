ARF COMMAND CENTER

Staff Portal.
Authorised Personnel Only.

This is the internal operations system ARF's team uses to run schools outreach, registrations, partnerships, finance and event logistics for MAKE IT MOVE 2026. It holds participant, school and financial data and is restricted to ARF team members signed in with an account issued by a Super Admin.

If you are a member of the public, a school, or a partner organisation, this system is not for you — please use Contact instead, or register for the event from the Make It Move 2026 page.
