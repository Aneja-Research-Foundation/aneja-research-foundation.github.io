THINK

Don't just have an opinion.
Defend it.

THINK is the intellectual arena of MAKE IT MOVE.

The flagship experience is our Model United Nations (MUN)—a structured diplomatic debate where participants represent countries, research international issues, negotiate, challenge opposing positions and work toward solutions.

It's not simply about knowing the facts.
It's about researching, reasoning, speaking and persuading.
