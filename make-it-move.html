WHAT IS MAKE IT MOVE

NOT JUST A
COMPETITION.

MAKE IT MOVE is a youth-led festival bringing together students across Bareilly to compete, create, perform, debate, research, build and solve. The festival combines creativity, academic excellence, technology and innovation under one platform.
