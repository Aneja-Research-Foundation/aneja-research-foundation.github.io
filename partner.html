PARTNER WITH ARF

Research is only
half the work.
Partners finish it.

ARF studies why infrastructure fails in Tier-2 Indian cities — and then tries to get the fix executed. That second half needs people with authority, access, data or resources. If you have any of those, there is a way to work with us.

Ways To Partner

Six kinds of partnership, each with a different thing to contribute. Most begin with a single conversation and one narrow pilot.

Government & Municipal Agencies

Take ARF's findings into the agencies that can act on them. The ARF-CIF framework is built for exactly this: one coordination committee, one zero-cost pilot, one monitoring tool — interventions sorted by cost and authority level, so a department can start with what it already has the power to do.

Schools & Colleges

Give students real research to do. ARF can run field-research modules, train a student research cell, or co-supervise projects with your faculty — observation, data collection, analysis and a written study, following the same five-stage method ARF uses.

Universities & Research Institutions

Co-author studies, review our methodology, or open up datasets and archives we cannot reach on our own. ARF publishes openly and credits every collaborator on the work itself.

Companies & Technical Partners

Contribute what research runs on: survey instruments, mapping and GIS tools, data infrastructure, hosting, printing, or engineering review of a proposed intervention. In-kind support is often worth more to us than money.

Funders, Trusts & CSR Programmes

Fund a defined study or a pilot rather than general operations: a corridor survey, an instrumented pilot at one junction, or the monitoring tool that tells everyone whether it worked. Every rupee is reported against a named output.

Individual Mentors & Advisors

Urban planners, civil engineers, administrators, academics and journalists who will read a draft honestly and tell a student team where the argument is weak. A few hours a month changes the quality of the work.

What A Partner Gets

ARF is a student-led foundation, not an agency. What we can offer is work, evidence and attribution — stated plainly.

01 Field evidence from the ground, not desk estimates
02 Published studies that name every collaborator
03 Interventions costed and sorted by authority level
04 A student team that does the legwork
05 Honest reporting, including what did not work

How It Works

No long proposals to begin with. Four steps from first email to a result someone can point at.

STEP 01

A conversation

Email us what you work on and what you would want out of this. A call or a meeting in Bareilly, thirty minutes, no commitment.

STEP 02

One narrow thing

We agree on a single defined piece of work — one corridor, one question, one dataset — small enough to finish and specific enough to judge.

STEP 03

The work, in writing

Scope, who does what, what gets shared and what stays confidential, and the date it is due. Short, and in plain language.

STEP 04

A published result

A study, a pilot or a tool — released publicly with your contribution credited, unless you ask us not to.

Questions Partners Ask

Who actually does the work?

Students, with faculty advice. That is the point of ARF: young researchers doing real field work, held to a method rather than to their age.

Do we have to fund anything?

No. Access, data, expertise, equipment and introductions are all real contributions — and often harder to come by than funding.

Can findings stay private?

Raw data and anything commercially sensitive can stay confidential, and we will say so up front. ARF's conclusions, though, are published — including the uncomfortable ones. That is what makes them worth having.

How long does a project take?

A focused study runs over a few months of field work and analysis. A zero-cost pilot can start much sooner. We would rather agree a realistic date than an impressive one.

Tell us what you’re working on.

One email is enough to start. Say who you are, what you work on, and what you would like to see fixed — we will reply with what ARF can realistically do.
